Daily targets

The default sodium target is 2,000 mg a day, the common WHO / Singapore HPB guideline. Set the numbers your own doctor or dietitian gave you if they differ.

Everything runs on your phone: your photos are never uploaded, and the log stays in this app’s storage. The model only knows these four dishes, so it always picks one of them, and a high percentage does not guarantee it is right. Calorie and sodium figures are estimates for a typical serving, not measurements; real bowls vary a lot, especially in sodium (the broth and sauce hold most of it). This is a tracking aid, not medical advice.
